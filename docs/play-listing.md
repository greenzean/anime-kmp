## Title
Anime Tracker

## Short description
Track your anime offline. Sync across devices and with MyAnimeList.

## Full description
Anime Tracker keeps your anime list on your phone, so it works without an internet connection and without an account.

- Search MyAnimeList and add anime to your library.
- Track status, episodes watched and your score.
- Search and filter your library, and sort it your way.
- Import and export MyAnimeList files.
- Optional: sign in with Google to sync your library between your devices.
- Optional: connect MyAnimeList to import your list and send changes back. Your MyAnimeList login and tokens stay on your device and are never sent to our server.
- Delete your account and all synced data at any time, in the app or on the web.

Anime data comes from MyAnimeList. Anime Tracker is an unofficial, non-commercial open source app and is not affiliated with MyAnimeList.

Source code: https://github.com/greenzean/anime-kmp
Privacy policy: https://greenzean.github.io/privacy/

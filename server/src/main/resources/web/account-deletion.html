Delete your Anime Tracker account

You can delete your Anime Tracker account and all data our server holds about you at any time, here or in the app. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your account on this page

Sign in with the Google account you use in Anime Tracker. We only use it to find and delete your account.

This deletes your account, your synced library and every sign-in session. It cannot be undone.

Deletion on this page is not available right now. Use the steps in the app below.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Anime Tracker on your Android device and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Go to Settings and choose Delete account.
  3. Read the summary, confirm, and approve the Google sign-in that proves the account is yours.

Open the delete screen in the app

What is deleted

  • Your account on the server, identified only by your Google account ID.
  • Your synced library: every entry (anime and status) and every deletion marker.
  • Every sign-in session of your account, on all devices.

What is kept

  • The copy of your library stored on your own device stays there until you remove it or uninstall the app.
  • Server logs and database backups may hold technical records, such as request times, for a short period before they are rotated out. They are not linked to your library.
  • Your data on MyAnimeList is not touched. This app never stores it on the server.
